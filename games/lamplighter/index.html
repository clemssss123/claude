The Lamplighter

Every night the shades crawl out of the dark. Light the lamps of Ashgrove, keep your own flame alive, and hold out until the sun comes up.

The lamplighter’s handbook

How to keep a town lit

Walk your round

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere to steer. You can also hold the mouse button to walk toward the pointer.

Your light burns them

Shades caught in your lantern light smoulder and slow down. Out in the dark you can only see their eyes.

Mind the oil

Your lantern drinks oil. As it runs low, your light shrinks. When it runs dry, your flame starts to gutter. Embers and flasks refill it.

Light the lamps

Stand by a dark street lamp for a moment to light it. Lit lamps burn shades on their own, and standing near one refills your oil and flame.

Grow stronger

Banished shades leave embers. Gather enough and you pick a new tool or a better lantern. Silver you earn buys lasting upgrades at the guild hall.

Last until dawn

The clock tower tolls every hour. The night gets worse as it goes on. At three o’clock, something enormous comes. Sunrise is at six.
