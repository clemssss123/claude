# The Lamplighter

A night-shift survival game in one HTML file. You are the lamplighter of
Ashgrove: walk the streets from 9 PM to dawn, light the street lamps, and keep
your own lantern burning while the shades close in. Your light is your weapon.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no dependencies; all
sound is synthesised with Web Audio.

- **Move:** WASD / arrow keys, drag on a touchscreen, hold the mouse button, or a gamepad
- **Pause:** Esc or P. **Mute:** M. **Level-up choices:** 1 / 2 / 3
- Nine in-game hours of about 72 seconds each; a boss arrives at 3 AM
- 12 upgrades to pick from as you level, plus a guild hall of permanent
  upgrades bought with silver between rounds (saved in `localStorage`)
