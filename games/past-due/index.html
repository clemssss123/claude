    Echoes

    Every life you finish is kept as an echo. An echo replays that life's own earnings, second by second, from the moment your current life begins, scaled to your current karma and Soul Tree so the race is fair. It pays you a share of what it earned at the same age. You get a bonus for every echo you are out-earning, and an echo you outlive counts as beaten.

    Soul Tree

    Karma carries over from life to life. Spend it here at any time. Each point of karma you have ever earned also adds 1% to all income, with diminishing returns past +100%. Your karma total is the cube root of everything you have ever earned, in thousands, so each life adds what it lifts that total by.
